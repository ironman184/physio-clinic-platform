## What this file helps decide

Whether booking needs an account, how reception-created records are connected to online access, and what staff versus patients can see. The comparison below reports documented patterns; it does not recommend one universal login method.

## Product-design implications, not approved requirements

Keep patient identity separate from a login method, a family/dependent relationship and a clinic's clinical record. The original recommendations discuss [phone-code access and durable patient identifiers](../common-patterns.md#use-phone-otp-as-access-not-as-the-patient-record). Account recovery and duplicate matching need their own tests.

## Validation route

Use [test 1 and the security questions](../demo-checklist.md#a-demo-that-reveals-the-real-system). Test an existing reception-created record, changed phone number and parent/child access; do not equate a successful sign-in with permission to every record.
