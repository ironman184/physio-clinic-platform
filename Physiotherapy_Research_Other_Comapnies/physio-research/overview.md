*10 platforms, their real workflows, and the patterns worth learning*

**The main conclusion.** A clinic-management product is not just a booking calendar. The useful unit is a treatment episode: patient identity, planned visits, therapist availability, clinical work, money and communication must remain consistent when something changes. The examples in this report show different ways to solve that problem. [[NO08](companies/nookal.md#source-no08), [ZA11](companies/zanda.md#source-za11), [HA10](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha10), [SP06](companies/splose.md#source-sp06)]

**The best references depend on the job.** Study Cliniko for a focused administration product; Jane for the patient relationship; Nookal, Zanda and splose for allied-health workflow detail; TM3 for referrals and linked booking; Halaxy for free-core economics and discovery. WebPT, Prompt and Raintree are especially useful references for insurance-heavy rehabilitation operations. These are research judgments, not measured rankings. [[CL14](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl14), [JA03](companies/jane.md#source-ja03), [NO03](companies/nookal.md#source-no03), [ZA14](companies/zanda.md#source-za14), [SP01](companies/splose.md#source-sp01), [TM01](companies/tm3.md#source-tm01), [HA01](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha01), [WE02](companies/webpt.md#source-we02), [PR02](companies/prompt.md#source-pr02), [RA01](companies/raintree.md#source-ra01)]

**Three details that change a product decision.** Cliniko documents patient cancellation but not direct patient rescheduling. Zanda and splose limit self-rescheduling to the same therapist, service and location. Prompt's own pages disagree about whether patients can reschedule online. A generic feature checklist would hide all three distinctions. [[CL09](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl09), [ZA05](companies/zanda.md#source-za05), [SP05](companies/splose.md#source-sp05), [PR02](companies/prompt.md#source-pr02), [PR03](companies/prompt.md#source-pr03)]

**How to read the evidence.** Numbered source codes are clickable. Help guides support the documented workflows; product pages support advertised capabilities, not proven outcomes. Version, region, plan and roadmap qualifications are kept next to the relevant feature. A demo check marks an unresolved detail, not proof that a feature is absent. No authenticated account or real patient transaction was tested.

Start with the shortlist below, then use the [index](INDEX.md) to choose a workflow or company profile. [Common patterns](common-patterns.md) keeps the original synthesis and product recommendations together.

## The ten-platform shortlist

This is a curated global shortlist, not a verified ranking by revenue, customers or market share. Selection prioritizes physiotherapy relevance, management depth, documented workflows and useful differences in product or business model. Exercise-only apps and generic calendars are outside this scope.

| **Platform** | **Best lens for this research** | **Why it belongs** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cliniko](companies/cliniko.md) | Straightforward clinic administration | Guest booking, core feature parity and managed migration. [[CL01](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl01), [CL03](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl03), [CL15](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl15)] |
| [Jane](companies/jane.md) | Patient experience and clinic growth | Portal, mobile app, exercise integration and marketing products. [[JA02](companies/jane.md#source-ja02), [JA03](companies/jane.md#source-ja03), [JA05](companies/jane.md#source-ja05)] |
| [Nookal](companies/nookal.md) | Case-based allied-health clinics | Case/service scheduling and tiered patient-portal access. [[NO08](companies/nookal.md#source-no08), [NO06](companies/nookal.md#source-no06)] |
| [Zanda](companies/zanda.md) | Recurring visits and package rules | Detailed series-edit, portal and financial behaviour. [[ZA05](companies/zanda.md#source-za05), [ZA06](companies/zanda.md#source-za06), [ZA11](companies/zanda.md#source-za11)] |
| [TM3](companies/tm3.md) | Referral-connected physiotherapy | Pronto, insurer workflows and newer guided Connect booking. [[TM01](companies/tm3.md#source-tm01), [TM03](companies/tm3.md#source-tm03), [TM10](companies/tm3.md#source-tm10)] |
| [WebPT](companies/webpt.md) | US rehabilitation operations | Resource and authorization-aware scheduling with billing. [[WE02](companies/webpt.md#source-we02), [WE07](companies/webpt.md#source-we07)] |
| [Prompt](companies/prompt.md) | Connected rehab operations | Task-based patient access, work queues and growth tools. [[PR04](companies/prompt.md#source-pr04), [PR02](companies/prompt.md#source-pr02), [PR07](companies/prompt.md#source-pr07)] |
| [Raintree](companies/raintree.md) | Enterprise rehabilitation | Complex scheduling, engagement and an evolving AI layer. [[RA05](companies/raintree.md#source-ra05), [RA04](companies/raintree.md#source-ra04), [RA08](companies/raintree.md#source-ra08)] |
| [Halaxy](companies/halaxy.md) | Free-core and directory model | Public discovery, configurable booking and paid usage. [[HA01](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha01), [HA02](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha02), [HA04](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha04)] |
| [splose](companies/splose.md) | Modern allied-health operations | Appointments versus support activities and funded-care focus. [[SP01](companies/splose.md#source-sp01), [SP06](companies/splose.md#source-sp06)] |

Two important naming distinctions: Zanda is the renamed Power Diary; they are one entry. TM3 is a Blue Zinc product, while Pronto is a related referral-network product. [[ZA02](companies/zanda.md#source-za02), [TM01](companies/tm3.md#source-tm01)]
