## Selling software versus attracting patients

This comparison separates how a vendor acquires clinics from tools that help a clinic acquire patients. These are not the same business. The table is a condensed reading guide; the linked company sections retain the full detail and caveats. It does not establish the effectiveness of any marketing channel.

| Platform | How it sells to clinics | Business distinction to study |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cliniko](companies/cliniko.md#how-it-markets-and-reduces-switching-friction) | Simplicity, transparent prices, educational resources, human support and managed migration. | Same core feature set across practitioner-count tiers; distinguish managed migration from narrower self-service import. ([CL01](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl01), [CL02](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl02), [CL15](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl15)) |
| [Jane](companies/jane.md#how-it-markets-itself-and-its-customers) | Practitioner education, webinars, community, ambassadors and customer stories. | Clinic-facing website, SEO and review products are separate from how Jane wins software customers; patient discovery has regional/beta limits. ([JA02](companies/jane.md#source-ja02), [JA13](companies/jane.md#source-ja13)) |
| [Nookal](companies/nookal.md#how-it-markets-and-positions-the-business) | Profession-specific pages, demos, educational material and AI-assisted workflow positioning. | Tiered operations product; Patient Portal is on Professional/Enterprise, with region-specific pricing. ([NO01](companies/nookal.md#source-no01), [NO02](companies/nookal.md#source-no02), [NO03](companies/nookal.md#source-no03), [NO06](companies/nookal.md#source-no06)) |

## Pricing: compare the whole cost

Published prices below are snapshots, not quotes. Currency, practitioner definition, annual commitment, tax, country and optional products differ. No currency conversion is used. A low headline fee does not establish the lowest total cost.

| **Platform** | **Published base / buying route** | **What to check beyond the headline** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cliniko | USD $45/mo solo; $95 for 2-5 practitioners. | Higher practitioner-count tiers; SMS $0.10/message; external services. [[CL01](companies/cliniko.md#source-cl01)] |
| Jane | US: Balance $54, Practice $79, Thrive $99/mo starting prices. | Balance volume limit; additional practitioners; AI, insurance and website products. [[JA01](companies/jane.md#source-ja01)] |
| Nookal | USD $65 Essentials / $89 Professional per practitioner/mo. | Regional tariffs differ; portal on higher plans; SMS/AI and enterprise scope. [[NO01](companies/nookal.md#source-no01), [NO06](companies/nookal.md#source-no06)] |
| Zanda | USD $19 Starter; Growth $49 base + $19 per additional practitioner/mo. | Plan restrictions; SMS and optional services. Do not compare promotional pricing with standard prices. [[ZA01](companies/zanda.md#source-za01)] |
| TM3 | Current numeric base price not established from reviewed official sources. | Obtain a module-level quote for Connect, referral/insurance workflows, communications and migration. [[TM01](companies/tm3.md#source-tm01), [TM03](companies/tm3.md#source-tm03)] |
| WebPT | Starter / Enhanced / Ultimate; numeric quote needed. | Confirm documentation, billing, claims, intake and engagement inclusion. [[WE06](companies/webpt.md#source-we06), [WE07](companies/webpt.md#source-we07)] |
| Prompt | Numeric quote needed. | Separate core EMR from Plus, Kiosk and other products in the offer. [[PR02](companies/prompt.md#source-pr02), [PR07](companies/prompt.md#source-pr07), [PR08](companies/prompt.md#source-pr08)] |
| Raintree | Numeric quote needed. | Implementation, interfaces, training, engagement and AI scope matter. [[RA01](companies/raintree.md#source-ra01), [RA04](companies/raintree.md#source-ra04), [RA08](companies/raintree.md#source-ra08)] |
| Halaxy | Core advertised as free. | Usage and transaction charges: SMS, payments, some claims, telehealth and AI. [[HA02](companies/halaxy.md#source-ha02)] |
| splose | Australia: AUD $30/mo; annual-plan equivalent $27, ex GST. | From 1 Nov 2026: $36 / $32 for new sign-ups and renewals. AI, SMS and payments remain separate cost items. [[SP02](companies/splose.md#source-sp02), [SP03](companies/splose.md#source-sp03)] |

**Use one comparable quote.** Give every vendor the same clinic scenario: practitioner count, locations, monthly visits, reminder volume, payment volume, imports and required integrations. Ask for year-one and renewal costs, the cancellation notice, data-export scope and the price of adding another clinic. This is a proposed buying method, not a vendor price claim.
