## Patient and scheduling workflow

### Where it fits

Prompt is a useful benchmark for a rehabilitation practice that wants documentation, scheduling, billing, analytics and patient engagement in a connected workflow. Its product family includes the EMR and separate products such as Plus and Kiosk. The boundaries matter: a feature advertised elsewhere on the website is not automatically included in the core contract. [[PR01][PR01], [PR02][PR02], [PR07][PR07], [PR08][PR08]]

### Staff login and patient identity

There is a separate staff login surface. Public patient-management material emphasizes avoiding a traditional password-heavy portal. The inspected patient contact-confirmation screen asks for an email address or mobile number plus date of birth, then sends a confirmation code. This supports a code-based access pattern; it does not prove every patient action uses an identical authentication sequence. [[PR10][PR10], [PR04][PR04], [PR06][PR06]]

### New patient and booking journey

Patients can reach forms, booking and payment tasks through links. Online scheduling can be configured for direct booking or for staff approval of a request. Availability depends on clinic rules, provider capacity and other visit constraints. Registration and insurance information can be collected digitally instead of re-entered from a paper form at the front desk. [[PR04][PR04], [PR05][PR05], [PR03][PR03]]

### Repeat treatment and the staff workflow

The scheduling materials describe support for plans of care and multiple visits. Version 10 promotes bulk creation and modification of recurring schedules with conflict checks. Because this is release material, confirm that the relevant tools are enabled in the offered account and demonstrate how they handle a single-date exception rather than relying on the announcement alone. [[PR03][PR03], [PR09][PR09]]

### Important contradiction: patient rescheduling

The EMR product page says patients can book or reschedule online. The scheduling FAQ says patients call the clinic to cancel or reschedule. Both are official statements. The correct conclusion is unresolved scope or documentation inconsistency, not a confident yes or no. Require a current demonstration, with the product version and module named. [[PR02][PR02], [PR03][PR03]]

### Check-in and next actions

Kiosk is a patient-facing check-in workflow available on a phone, tablet or computer. It can collect forms, insurance details and balances and help arrange future visits. This is not merely an arrival button: it moves unfinished administrative work into a guided patient task flow. Kiosk is a named product, so check its commercial inclusion. [[PR08][PR08]]

## Operations and business

### Notifications and schedule recovery

The scheduling page describes email and text reminders. Plus adds tools such as waitlist matching, underbooked-patient follow-up and lead management. Underbooking means a patient has fewer future visits arranged than the intended treatment plan; it is different from a no-show. The product tries to address both open diary space and patients dropping out of care. [[PR03][PR03], [PR07][PR07]]

### Clinical work and billing

The core EMR positions documentation, billing, clearinghouse workflows and analytics together. Operational dashboards surface unfinished work such as unsigned notes or scheduling and eligibility issues. The practical advantage to test is whether a staff member can act from the alert and complete the task without re-entering the same patient or appointment information. [[PR02][PR02]]

### AI and new features

Version 10 promotes AI assistance around intake, documentation, eligibility and other administrative tasks, alongside scheduling changes. Treat these as product announcements and ask what is generally available, which tasks require human approval, what data is retained and what costs extra. The website is not independent evidence of accuracy, clinical benefit or a guaranteed productivity gain. [[PR09][PR09]]

### Website and application design

The patient design is task-centred: open the relevant link, verify identity when required and finish the booking, form or payment action. The staff design emphasizes an integrated work queue rather than separate lists with no next action. Engage is presented as a branded patient-experience product; that is separate from assuming every patient must install an app. [[PR04][PR04], [PR05][PR05], [PR02][PR02]]

### Marketing and commercial model

Prompt sells a combination of operating efficiency and practice growth, using product demonstrations, specialist content and customer examples. Plus provides lead capture, follow-up campaigns, surveys and review-related workflows for clinics. A dependable public numeric base price was not established in the reviewed official pages; request a quote separating core EMR, Plus, Kiosk and other options. [[PR01][PR01], [PR07][PR07]]

### Strongest lesson and demo checks

The useful lesson is to connect an operational problem with a next action: missing form, unsigned note, empty slot or patient without follow-up. For procurement, resolve the self-rescheduling contradiction in writing, test the patient confirmation-code flow, inspect reminder delivery and opt-out handling, and verify the recurring-edit and financial consequences of a cancellation. [[PR02][PR02], [PR03][PR03], [PR06][PR06], [PR09][PR09]]
