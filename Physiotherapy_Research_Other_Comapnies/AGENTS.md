# Physiotherapy product research

For physiotherapy product-design tasks, start with
`docs/physio-research/INDEX.md` and read the relevant linked files.

Treat this research as reference, not approved product requirements.
Preserve source links and uncertainty labels.
Do not assume undocumented features exist.

Use workflow files for focused comparisons and company files for full detail.
Read the relevant sections rather than loading the entire research folder.
Keep existing project requirements authoritative; do not silently replace them
with a competitor's rules or an unapproved recommendation.

This research is dated 25 September 2026. Keep region, plan, version and roadmap
qualifications. When a task depends on current vendor behaviour or pricing,
re-check the cited official source; do not present this snapshot as a new check.

Distinguish confirmed bookings from requests, staff actions from patient actions,
and appointment changes from refunds or session-credit changes.
